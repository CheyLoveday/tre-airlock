Honest scope — a quick MVP

!This is a deliberately scoped MVP on synthetic data.

The point is the controls, failure modes, and governance — not a production service. Run against real OFH resources, I'd expect edge cases to surface. The design anticipates them; real data is where they get stress-tested:

  • Palindromic strand (C/G, A/T) at common MAF, where AF concordance is ambiguous.
  • Indel imputation & array indel calling — the CHEK2 c.1100delC case.
  • Build / liftover and reference mismatch on back-formed CPRAs.
  • Multi-allelic loci and allele-normalization corner cases.
  • Ancestry-sparse strata & small-cell differencing under SDC.
  • DR2 thresholds at the credible-vs-conditional boundary.
  • Scale — the full ~159M-variant imputed resource, not a fixture.

These are flagged in the pre-flight, the caveats, and the governance gates — but I'd treat first real-data runs as a discovery exercise, surfacing and hardening the difficult edge cases, rather than a finished service.
