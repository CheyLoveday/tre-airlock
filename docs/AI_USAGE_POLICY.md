# AI Usage Policy

AI tools may be used to accelerate software development, testing, documentation, and workflow design.
They are NOT an authority channel and do not replace study approval, TRE controls, governance review, or
human sign-off.

## Allowed
- Drafting code structure, refactors, and tests against SYNTHETIC data.
- Generating SOPs, ADRs, and stakeholder-note templates.
- Explaining APIs, statistical methods, and package behaviour.
- Scaffolding profiling scripts and CI configuration.

## Prohibited
- Uploading or pasting participant-level data, pseudonymous IDs, raw TRE outputs, screenshots, secrets,
  or unpublished governance-sensitive material into external AI systems.
- Treating AI-generated text as institutional policy without evidence.
- Using AI output as final analytical truth without validation.

## Validation requirement
All AI-assisted artefacts are DRAFT until they have passed human review, version-controlled
reproduction, appropriate tests, and governance / output checks. For substantial AI-assisted changes,
an append-only project note records task, files changed, validation performed, and unresolved
assumptions.

## Scope
This is an internal engineering control under OFH governance (registered-researcher access, TRE
analysis, Five Safes, controlled airlock export). It is not OFH institutional policy and must not be
presented as such.
